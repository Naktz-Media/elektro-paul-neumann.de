The use of these technically necessary cookies is based on Art.6 Abs.1 lit.f DSGVO (legitimate interest in a functioning website). Consent is therefore not required.

Use of YouTube components with enhanced privacy mode

elektro-paul-neumann.de uses components (videos) from YouTube, LLC 901 Cherry Ave., 94066 San Bruno, CA, USA, a company of Google Inc., Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA.

We use the "enhanced privacy mode" option provided by YouTube. When you visit a page that contains an embedded video, a connection to the YouTube servers is established, and the content is displayed on the website by sending a message to your browser.

According to YouTube, in "enhanced privacy mode," only data is transmitted to the YouTube server, specifically which of our websites you visited when watching the video. If you are logged in to YouTube at the same time, this information will be assigned to your YouTube account. You can prevent this by logging out of your account before visiting our website.

To comply with the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), YouTube videos are blocked by default on this website. Instead, a placeholder with a notice appears.
