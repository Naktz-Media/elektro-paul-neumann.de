Smart Home

Intelligent building technology for greater comfort, security and efficiency

A Smart Home stands for intelligent building automation and the networking of technical devices and systems in living spaces and buildings. The goal is to increase living comfort, improve energy efficiency, and simultaneously enhance security – all based on automated processes and remotely controllable components.

Smart HomeGet advice

With tablet, smartphone or PC

Control your building from anywhere in the world

Thanks to modern smart home technology, numerous functions can be conveniently controlled via smartphone, tablet or PC:

  • Lighting control including individually customizable lighting scenes and schedules
  • Automated blinds and roller shutter control
  • Remote access to video surveillance and alarm systems
  • Intelligent heating control for maximum energy savings
  • Status messages for security-relevant changes or events

Whether in a new building or an existing property, many systems can be retrofitted without much effort. All that's required is a Wi-Fi connection. Control is conveniently via a user-friendly app or software solution.
