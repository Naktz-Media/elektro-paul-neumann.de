Statistical arguments for video surveillance

Burglary figures show: The danger is real. According to the insurance industry, the number of residential burglaries has increased significantly in recent years. Particularly worrying: In approximately 20% of cases the residents were present during the crime – in some cases there was even direct contact with the perpetrators.

Even more serious are often the long-term consequences:

  • Almost half of those affected no longer feel safe in their own home, even one year after the burglary.
  • One in four people considers moving house after the incident.
  • The average damage costs per case are over 3,000 euros.

A well-planned surveillance system can not only deterrence, but also to enlightenment– and thus actively contributes to the protection of your family and values.

Which system suits you?

Wireless or wired

Modern video surveillance systems offer flexible installation options:

  • Wireless variants are ideal for rental apartments or places with frequently changing locations.
  • Wired systems guarantee maximum reliability and are particularly recommended for continuous operation.

We provide you with individual advice, inspect your property on-site in Cologne and the surrounding area, and recommend the right system – including the number of cameras, storage solution, and positioning.

Intelligent monitoring for peace of mind

Digital recordings with the highest image quality, night vision, and cloud storage are standard today. Real-time transmission ensures you stay informed about events at all times—even when you're on the go.

Modern camera systems are compact, discreet, and robust—making them virtually impossible to tamper with. This ensures your security at all times.
