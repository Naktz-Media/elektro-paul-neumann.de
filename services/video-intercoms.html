Video intercom systems

Uninvited visitors? With modern video intercom systems, you stay in control.

It's late in the evening, the doorbell rings, and you don't know who's at the door? Video door intercom systems offer a valuable added security feature in moments like these. Thanks to live video and two-way communication, you can immediately see who's at your front door or business premises—without having to open the door.

What distinguishes a video intercom system from conventional systems?

In contrast to conventional audio intercom systems, video door intercom systems offer visual security:

  • Real-time video transmission on an indoor display
  • Communication via camera and microphone
  • Integrated night vision thanks to infrared technology

The camera image appears on an indoor monitor. Many systems can now also be controlled via smartphone or tablet – for maximum flexibility and control, even while on the move.

Individual solutions for every need

From compact single-family homes to commercial properties – we offer video intercom systems in various designs:

  • Models with moving camera for a wide viewing angle
  • Systems with Motion detection and image storage that automatically creates recordings
  • High-end systems with App connection, ideal for mobile users

Our experts in Cologne, Germany and Mallorca will help you find the right model – including design advice for stylish integration into your surroundings.

Smart technology, intuitive operation

The systems we use rely on modern technologies and the highest quality standards:

  • High-resolution displays
  • Clear sound and low latency
  • Secure data connection and high durability

Regardless of whether you want to operate your system via touchscreen in the hallway or via smartphone app – we will show you which functions are really useful for your situation.

Security for all generations

A video door intercom system offers protection – for singles as well as for families with children or seniors. Especially for older people, it's reassuring to be able to visually identify visitors before the door is opened. Your family won't be left unprotected even while traveling or at work.
