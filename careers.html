Become part of the team

Career at Elektro Paul Neumann.

As a specialist company for electrical and security technology with demanding customers in Germany and Mallorca, we rely on team members who think for themselves, take responsibility – and are passionate about what they do.

Open positions Apply by email

What you bring with you

You enjoy working in a team, are reliable, flexible, and approach our customers with professionalism and friendliness. Technical understanding and manual dexterity are just as much a given for you as a willingness to develop yourself and think outside the box.

  • Completed training in electrical engineering
  • Ideally, initial experience in service, project management or on construction sites
  • Class B driving license
  • Desire for long-term cooperation

What we offer you

We offer you more than just a job – we offer you prospects. Look forward to a professional environment with high-quality projects, personal appreciation, and genuine future security.

  • Permanent position after successful probationary period
  • Attractive earning opportunities
  • High-quality service vehicle
  • Thorough training and collegial cooperation
  • Regular training – technical and safety-related
  • Social security and a modern working environment

We also welcome career changers with technical expertise. We have been committed to training for years and can proudly look back on numerous successes.
