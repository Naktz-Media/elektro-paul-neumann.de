Frequently asked questions

What is KNX?

KNX is the globally recognized standard (EN 50090 / ISO/IEC 14543) for the intelligent networking of home and building technology. It enables centralized, cross-manufacturer control of lighting, shading, heating, security, and more – efficiently, securely, and scalably.

Can KNX be retrofitted in existing buildings?

Yes. In addition to the traditional bus (TP), there are wireless and IP options. We will review the existing infrastructure and plan a suitable integration.

Do you handle maintenance and extensions?

Yes. We handle updates, error analysis, enhancements, and reprogramming – across all manufacturers and with long-term service in Cologne and the surrounding area.

How much does a KNX installation cost?

This depends on the scope (trades, sensors/actuators, visualization). After an on-site appointment, you will receive a binding quote with a detailed description of the work.

Why is it difficult to find good KNX electricians?

KNX requires certification (KNX partner), in-depth ETS knowledge, and interdisciplinary experience across multiple disciplines. Qualified integrators are specialized and therefore rare – we ensure this through certified expertise and numerous completed projects.
